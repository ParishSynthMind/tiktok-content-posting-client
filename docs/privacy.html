Privacy policy

Last updated 5 November 2026.

TikTok Content Posting Client runs only on the computer of the creator who installs it. It is used by that creator to post their own videos to their own TikTok account.

  • What it accesses: with your permission, your basic TikTok profile (username and display name) and the ability to upload and post videos you choose (scopes user.info.basic, video.upload, video.publish).
  • Where data is kept: access tokens are stored on your own computer (macOS Keychain or a private file). Captions and the approval queue are stored in local files. Nothing is sent to any server run by the developer.
  • Who it is shared with: your videos and captions are sent only to TikTok, and only after you approve them. Caption drafts are written by Anthropic's Claude API from a brief you write; the brief and draft are sent to Anthropic for that purpose only.
  • Deleting your data: run python -m tt_poster revoke, or remove the app in TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security, Manage app permissions, then delete the local files.

Questions: open an issue on the project's GitHub page.
