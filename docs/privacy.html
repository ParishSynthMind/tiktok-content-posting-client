Privacy policy

Last updated 17 November 2026.

This policy explains what Content Posting Client ("the app") does with your information when you connect your TikTok account to it.

What we access

  • Basic TikTok profile (scope user.info.basic): your open ID, display name and avatar, used to show which account a video will be sent to.
  • Video upload (scope video.upload): used only to upload a video you have approved to your TikTok inbox as a draft, and to check its upload status.
  • Information you provide: the brief you write, the video file you choose and the caption you approve.

We do not access your followers, messages, likes, comments, other videos or analytics.

How we use it

Only to draft a caption, show it to you for approval, and send the approved video and caption to TikTok. We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or build profiles.

Where it is stored and for how long

  • Access and refresh tokens are stored on your own computer, in the macOS Keychain or a private file only your user can read. They are kept until you revoke access or delete them.
  • Briefs, captions and the approval queue are local files on your computer. A posting log keeps only an ID, a fingerprint, the TikTok publish ID, the status and the settings you chose, with no caption text and no tokens.
  • No data is sent to, or stored on, any server run by the developer.

Who it is shared with

  • TikTok, to sign you in and to receive the video and caption you approved.
  • Anthropic (Claude API), which receives your brief to draft the caption. It does not receive your TikTok tokens or profile. You can skip this and write the caption yourself.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • Revoke access at any time with python -m tt_poster revoke, or in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy, Security, Manage app permissions.
  • Delete the local files and Keychain items to remove everything the app stored.

Children

The app is not intended for anyone under 18.
