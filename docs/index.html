Write it with AI. Approve it yourself. Post it the official way.

Content Posting Client is a free, open-source tool for creators. AI drafts a caption and hashtags for your video from a short brief, you check and approve it, and the video is sent to your own TikTok account through TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API. Nothing is ever posted without your approval.

How it works

  1. Sign in with TikTok. You connect your account on TikTok's own sign-in page and choose what to allow. You can remove access at any time.
  2. Write a brief. Topic, audience, tone and the facts the caption may use, plus the video file.
  3. AI drafts the caption. It is checked automatically: TikTok's length limit, no invented figures, no links or @mentions, no spammy hashtags.
  4. You review and approve. You see the account it will go to, the video, the caption, and you choose the privacy level and interaction settings yourself. Nothing is pre-selected.
  5. Sent to TikTok. The video is uploaded to your TikTok inbox as a draft. You open TikTok, make any final edits and post it from the TikTok app.

What it uses from TikTok

  • Login Kit (scope user.info.basic): to sign you in and show which account the video will go to (display name and avatar).
  • Content Posting API (scope video.upload): to upload the video you approved to your TikTok inbox as a draft, then show its progress until it arrives.

It does not read your followers, messages or other videos, and it never likes, follows, comments or messages on your behalf.

Your data

Your sign-in tokens, briefs and captions stay on your own computer. Videos and captions go only to TikTok, and only after you approve them. Full details are in the privacy policy.
