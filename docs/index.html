Write it with AI. Approve it yourself. Send it to TikTok the official way.

Content Posting Client turns a short brief into a ready-to-post TikTok caption, holds it for your approval, and then uploads your video to your TikTok inbox as a draft, using TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API. Nothing leaves your computer until you say so.

What it does

AI caption drafts

Give it the topic, audience, tone and the facts it may use. It drafts a caption and hashtags that stick to those facts.

Automatic checks

Every draft is checked against TikTok's length limit and for invented figures, links, @mentions and spammy hashtags before you see it.

Your approval, every time

Each caption waits in an approval queue. You edit, approve or reject it. Nothing is ever sent without your approval.

Official TikTok upload

Approved videos go to your TikTok inbox as a draft through the Content Posting API. You finish and post it in the TikTok app.

Your account, your control

You sign in on TikTok's own page and choose what to allow. You can remove access at any time.

Open source

The full code is public under the MIT licence, with tests that run on every change.

How it works

  1. Sign in with TikTok. The app opens TikTok's sign-in page. You log in there (the app never sees your password), review the two permissions it asks for and choose whether to allow them.
  2. Write a brief. Topic, audience, tone and the facts the caption may use, plus the video file you want to share.
  3. AI drafts the caption. The draft is checked automatically and goes into the approval queue.
  4. Review and approve. The approval screen shows the TikTok account it will go to (your TikTok name), the video, the caption, and the settings. You edit the caption if you like and approve or reject it.
  5. Upload to your TikTok inbox. The approved video is uploaded as a draft. The app shows its progress until TikTok confirms it has arrived. You open TikTok, make any final edits and post it yourself.

Your data

  • Your TikTok sign-in tokens are kept on your own computer (in the macOS Keychain or a private file) and are never sent to us.
  • Briefs, captions and the approval queue are local files on your computer.
  • Your video and caption go only to TikTok, and only after you approve them. The brief is sent to Anthropic's Claude API to draft the caption; you can skip that and write the caption yourself.
  • We run no server that stores your data, we do not sell data and we show no ads.

Frequently asked questions

Does it post anything without asking me?

No. Every caption waits in the approval queue, and even after you approve it the video arrives in your TikTok inbox as a draft that you post yourself.

Does the app see my TikTok password?

No. You sign in on TikTok's own page. The app only receives a sign-in token, which stays on your computer.

Which TikTok permissions does it ask for?

Two: user.info.basic (your basic profile, used to show your TikTok name) and video.upload (to send your approved video to your inbox as a draft).

How do I disconnect it?

Run python -m tt_poster revoke, or in the TikTok app go to Settings and privacy, Security, Manage app permissions, and remove Content Posting Client. Then delete the local files.

Do I have to use the AI captions?

No. You can write or paste your own caption and still use the approval queue and the upload.

What does it cost?

The app is free and open source. If you use AI drafting you need your own Anthropic API key, billed by Anthropic.
